Business Context

Export Trade Africa is an import, export and trading company based in Lekki, Lagos, Nigeria. The website needs to read as international and credible to a buyer in Europe, the US, or Asia who has never met the company in person, while still being clean and fast for someone on a Nigerian mobile connection.

Design Direction

Approach this as the design lead at a small studio, not a template generator. Every choice below should feel specific to this business, not like a default.

Avoid the three looks that AI generated sites default to unless there is a real reason for one: a cream background with a high contrast serif and terracotta accent, a near black background with one neon accent, and a broadsheet newspaper layout with hairline rules and zero border radius. None of these suit a trade and logistics company, so none get used automatically.

Color: pick 4 to 6 named colors with hex values. A deep primary tied to trust, deep green or deep navy both work well for trade, one warm accent such as amber or terracotta used sparingly, one or two neutrals for text, one neutral for backgrounds and surfaces.

Typography: a display face used with restraint for headlines that has real character, not the default corporate sans, paired with a highly readable body face. No more than three faces total including a utility face for captions and form labels if needed.

Layout: describe the layout in one sentence per page before building it. Any structural device such as numbered steps only belongs where the content is genuinely sequential, for example the import and export process. A product grid does not need numbers.

Signature element: pick one thing this site will be remembered by and put the design effort there, keeping everything else quiet and disciplined around it. A strong option here is a full width, high quality photography treatment on the hero and product sections, since real imagery is the trust signal for a trade business.

Imagery: use real photography throughout, warehouse, product handling, shipping, people. No stock photography, no AI generated images, no generic globe icons or handshake illustrations. Where a real photo is not available yet for a section, leave a clearly labeled placeholder rather than a fake image.

International polish: copy should read as professional international business English, no slang, no filler. Include country and currency neutral language where pricing is discussed since buyers may be anywhere. If a certification or registration number is shown, format it clearly and prominently since that is what builds trust with an overseas buyer who cannot visit in person.

Accessibility and quality floor: strong color contrast, visible focus states on every interactive element, semantic HTML structure, alt text on every image, forms with clear labels and inline validation, fully responsive from a small phone up through a large desktop screen.

Pages, Fully Detailed

Home

Hero: full width real photo or photo composition of an actual operation, a clear headline stating what the company does and for whom, one primary call to action to request a quote and one secondary call to action to view products.

Trust bar directly under the hero: years in operation, number of countries served, key certifications shown as small clean logos or badges.

Three service tiles: import, export, sourcing, each with a short one line description and a link into the Services page.

Featured products: a short row of 3 to 4 product categories with a real photo per category and a link into Products.

Why work with us: three to four short points covering reliability, direct sourcing, documentation and compliance, and responsive communication, each with a small supporting line, not just a headline.

Testimonial: one or two real client quotes with name, company, and country, presented cleanly, not as generic filler text.

Closing call to action: a final prompt to request a quote, repeated once more before the footer.

Footer: certifications, physical address, phone, email, WhatsApp link, social links, quick links to every main page.

About

The company's history and what it does in plain language, no corporate jargon.

Leadership or team introduction with real photos and names, since an overseas buyer wants to know who they are dealing with.

Mission stated plainly, one paragraph, not a vague slogan.

A short section on where the company operates from, with the physical address and an embedded map.

Products

A clean grid of product categories, each with a real photo, a short description, and a link into a detail page.

Each product detail page includes: full description, specifications, minimum order quantity, packaging information, and a call to action to request a quote for that specific product.

Services

Four clearly separated service sections: import, export, sourcing, and warehousing and logistics. Each explained in plain language covering what it includes, who it is for, and how the process works, with a short step by step breakdown where the process genuinely has steps.

Get a Quote

A clean form asking for product, quantity, destination country, target price, and timeline.

A short reassurance line near the form stating expected response time.

A confirmation screen after submission stating the request was received and when to expect a reply, plus a WhatsApp link as an alternative way to reach the company immediately.

Become a Supplier

A short explanation of what the company looks for in a supplier.

A form capturing what they supply, their location, and their volume capacity.

A confirmation screen after submission.

Trust and Compliance

Certifications and registration details shown clearly, including business registration and any export authority certificates.

A short section explaining how payments and transactions are handled to reassure an overseas buyer who has not worked with the company before.

Contact

Phone, WhatsApp click to open a chat, email, physical address with an embedded map, and business hours.

A short contact form as an alternative to the direct channels above.
